# The Free-Improv Arbitrage

Indefinite improvisation is a rare, monetizable skill most pianists can't
offer. That's the arbitrage: there are markets that pay for *exactly* the
thing you can already do — vamp till ready, for an hour, on demand — and they
are chronically undersupplied.

## The markets

- **Dance-class accompaniment.** Ballet and modern programs need improvising
  accompanists for technique classes — steady, hourly, institutional work.
  This is the anchor: the **OU School of Dance** provides live accompaniment
  for *all* technique classes and staffs multiple accompanists, and **UCO** and
  **OCU** run large programs too. Undersupplied, recurring, and it pays weekly.
- **Silent-film and museum event scoring.** The **Oklahoma City Museum of Art**
  and the **First Americans Museum** both program public events and film — live
  piano scoring is a natural pitch.
- **Yoga / meditation / sound-bath live music.** Studios built around sound
  healing (**Sound Bath Studio OKC**, **This Land Yoga**, **The Yoga Spa**,
  **3rd Street Yoga**) already run the format your improvisation fits.
- **Gallery openings and art walks.** Ambient improv is perfect for openings —
  **First Friday** on the Paseo is 25+ galleries in one night.
- **Theater underscoring and improv-comedy accompaniment.** **OKC Improv** runs
  weekly shows; **Lyric**, **Carpenter Square**, and **Civic Center** all use
  live and rehearsal music. Musical-improv accompaniment is a specialty almost
  no one else in town offers.

## The pitch

Lead with the skill they can't easily hire: *"I improvise continuously and read
the room in real time — for class, for the mat, for the gallery floor."* For
dance programs, ask for the accompanist coordinator by name and offer to sub a
single class first. For studios and galleries, offer one pilot session at your
floor rate; the recurring booking is the goal.
