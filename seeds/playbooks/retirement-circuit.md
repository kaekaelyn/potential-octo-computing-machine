# The Retirement Circuit

Activity directors at retirement and assisted-living communities book
entertainment *every month*, from a real budget, with almost no friction. There
is no audition, no committee, no degree wall — there is a calendar with empty
squares and a director whose job is to fill them.

## The math

Land **8–10 communities** on a rotating monthly route and you have a steady
income floor that funds everything else. One 45–60 minute set of standards,
sing-alongs, and familiar songs, repeated across ten facilities, is ten
bookings a month from one hour of prepared material. Because it rotates, no
single community sees you more than monthly, and the route compounds:
directors talk to each other.

## The route

Treat the circuit as a trackable route with per-facility cadence. Seeded
communities (**Saint Ann**, **Concordia**, **The Harrison**, **Elison
Statesman Club**, **Storey Oaks**, and more from the Overpass import) are tagged
**retirement-circuit**. For each:

1. Find the **activity / life-enrichment director** — that's the booker. (Vamp
   never invents a contact; confirm the name before pitching.)
2. Pitch a monthly set with a themed hook (decades, holidays, Broadway).
3. Once booked, set the per-prospect cadence so the next month's ask is teed up
   automatically.

## The pitch

*"I bring a full monthly program of the songs your residents grew up with — no
setup burden on your staff, one flat rate, and I can theme it to your calendar."*
Directors rebook what's easy for them. Be easy.

## Rate

See the rate card's **Retirement / senior-living show** range as a starting
point (confirm locally — it's an unsourced estimate). Hold your floor; the value
is the *route*, not any single set.
