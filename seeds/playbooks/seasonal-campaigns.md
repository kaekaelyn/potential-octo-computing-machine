# Seasonal Campaigns

Booking runs months ahead of the calendar. By the time a holiday party is on
everyone's mind, it's booked. The money is in reaching out during the quiet
window *before* the season, when budgets are open and calendars are empty. This
playbook is a set of timed reminders so you're always pitching the season that's
coming, not the one that's here.

## The calendar

- **September–October → holiday corporate parties.** Companies plan December
  parties in the fall. Pitch event planners, country clubs, and hotels now for
  December cocktail-hour and party piano. (Cross-reference the **Planner Play**.)
- **January → wedding-planner outreach.** Planners build their vendor lists for
  the spring/summer season in January. This is when the **Planner Play**
  activates — get on the list before the season fills.
- **March–April & November–December → church sub blitz.** Easter and Christmas
  add services and burn out regulars. The **Sub List** playbook activates here —
  send seasonal availability to the whole worship-leader contact list.

## How Vamp helps

Each seasonal playbook carries its **active months**. When a playbook's month
arrives, it surfaces as a reminder (M7) — *"It's September: pitch holiday
corporate parties"* — and its tagged prospects are already in the pipeline,
ready to work. You don't have to remember the calendar; the calendar remembers
you.

## The rule

A seasonal pitch is still a personal pitch. Activation tells you *when*; the
angle on each prospect tells you *what to say*. Never blast a template — send
something you'd stand behind.
