# The Planner Play

For weddings and private events, don't pitch the two thousand couples. Pitch the
twenty planners. A couple books one wedding in their life; a wedding planner
books dozens a year and hands the ceremony-music decision to whoever they trust.
Win the planner once and you win a stream of referrals.

## The leverage

Wedding planners, corporate event planners, and venue coordinators are **repeat
referrers**. The relationship, not the gig, is the asset — so Vamp tracks them
differently: they graduate to the **people log** (M6) with a referral chain, so
you can see which planner actually produces bookings. Until then they live in
the pipeline tagged **planner-play**.

## The seeded twenty

Start with the seeded planners and event-forward venues (**Aisle Be With You**,
**Completely Coordinated**, **Gather Together**, **Cultivate**, **Gibson
Events**, and venues like **Gaillardia** and **Quail Creek** that book their own
weddings). Confirm each contact before pitching — several are `verified: false`.

## The pitch

Planners care about two things: you make them look good, and you never create a
problem. Lead with reliability and a clean referral experience:

*"I'm a ceremony + cocktail pianist who shows up early, dresses the part, and
makes your timeline easy. Here's a live video and a one-sheet you can forward to
couples — no work for you."*

Give them something **forwardable**: the EPK link and a live video. The easiest
referral is the one they can make by pasting a link.

## Timing

Booking runs months ahead of the season. This playbook activates in **January**
— outreach for the spring/summer wedding calendar, while planners are building
their vendor lists for the year.
